Prepare as if delivering to a client

Parent consult rehearsal

Practise calm, advisory language. Your client is usually a parent seeking clarity, confidence, and a plan — not another worksheet pack.

Consult flow (20 minutes)

0–3

Listen

Goals, history, what has been tried, current stress points.

3–8

Diagnose

Content gap vs skills vs organisation vs confidence/wellbeing.

8–15

Roadmap

2–4 week plan, ownership, checkpoints, measurement.

15–20

Close

Confirm realism, next touchpoint, parent questions.

Say-this scripts

Sample case: “Tutoring didn’t work”

  1. Validate effort already made.
  2. Ask: marks target, pathway goal, weekly capacity.
  3. Separate missing content from missing strategy.
  4. Propose diagnosis week + tracker + one skill focus.
  5. Explain when a tutor still helps (content catch-up only).

Language to avoid

  • “I’ll just teach the whole syllabus again.”
  • “Your child is lazy.”
  • Guaranteeing ATAR numbers.
  • Acting as psychologist/doctor.
  • Teacher-lounge jargon parents won’t follow.
Rehearse these out loud once a day before the interview.
