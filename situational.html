About Nandini

Hi my name is Nandini. I’m a secondary Mathematics teacher with experience of teaching across India, NZ and Australia. In India I taught Years 1 to 12, in NZ I taught Years 7 and 8 and I currently teach Years 9 Advanced, 10 Standard and 11 Advanced in Australia.

My major education is from India, where I completed bachelor's of commerce with honours in accountancy. I then studied graduate diploma in teaching from university of Auckland, NZ. As I received A+ in that program, the university offered me a research master scholarship to explore Indian parents experiences of their children’s mathematics education in New Zealand classroom.

During my teaching in New Zealand I completed a year-long professional development in Accelerated learning worked closely with below level students and helped them to reach the expected level. Later on I took a leadership role where I mentored my colleagues to implement strategies in mix-ability classrooms and helped to boost individual student achievement and overall whole school’s achievement. My classes consistently ranked in the top 3.

I believe all students come to school with two important things one is their understanding of the world and another is their potential to success. As a teacher my role is to consolidate and connect with the students understanding and inspire them to reach their own potential. My goal is to encourage and facilitate students to succeed in their educational experience and ultimately in the society itself.
