Day of interview

One-page cheat sheet

Read once before you join. Do not read from notes on camera.

30-second opening

“I’m a Stage 6 Mathematics leader and secondary maths teacher in NSW. I help Years 7–12 students improve through personalised diagnosis, efficient learning systems, and family partnership. What attracts me to Kalibrate-Ed is the focus on building capable, independent learners — strategy and mindset, not just more content.”

Superpower line

“My superpower is finding the real barrier — misconception, organisation, language load, or confidence — and building a personalised plan that grows both competence and independence.”

Always do

  • Diagnose before prescribing
  • Use checkpoints and ownership
  • Speak to parents as clients
  • End with a measurable result

Never do

  • Guarantee ATAR outcomes
  • Sound like a content-only tutor
  • Overshare classroom politics
  • Claim clinical expertise

Contractor ready

  • Willing to obtain ABN
  • Flexible remote delivery
  • Clear reporting rhythm
  • Professional boundaries

Closing question to ask Kelly

“What does excellent Education Strategist delivery look like in the first 30 days with a new family — and which student profiles are highest priority right now?”

Keep water nearby. Smile with your voice. Pause before answering.
